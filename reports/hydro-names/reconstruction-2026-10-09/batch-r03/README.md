- **40182409: Сарысу / Sarysu.** Qualified representative-system identity supported by primary geographic studies and official operational usage. Full-basin thematic maps supply regional context without individually labeling every reach. Exact headwater-name boundaries, seasonal terminal flow and lake-mouth membership remain unresolved. The intended map on the 2017 study’s printed page 158 is blank and was not treated as map evidence.

All Korean research names are null. No whole-group scalar name, per-reach name application or automatic product change is approved. Baseline roles, source IDs and terminal classes are distinct from naming evidence.

## Complete geometry inspection and its limits

All nine actual decoded baseline fragments were inspected, including every MultiLineString part: 678 line parts/source IDs and 4,220 coordinate positions (including repeated endpoints). Exact metadata joins, ordered identifier sequences and full rendered-geometry hashes are retained. Nura has 1,378 positions, Flinders 1,436 and Sarysu 1,406. Original continental HydroRIVERS shapefiles were not acquired, so equality to original per-reach coordinates and original reach-to-rendered-part mapping remain unverified.

The independent review checks current target source evidence and whole-group scope. Matching source-ID and line-part counts is not proof of individual source-reach geometry identity. No geometry repair or comprehensive topology reconstruction was performed.

## Sources, publication and reproduction

The three findings/evidence pairs contain URLs, source dates, source roles, concise own factual extraction, locators, exact capture or original-body hashes and naming limits. Nura uses four exact tool text captures with explicitly unavailable raw-PDF hashes. Flinders and Sarysu use six hashed original response bodies. Raw source articles, reports, maps, screenshots and crops are omitted.

HydroRIVERS rights differ from the public-domain Natural Earth lake data used in earlier batches. No full river coordinate arrays, selected GeoJSON or binary packs are distributed here. `geometry-publication-rights.json` records official product/license sources and conservative limits. Public identifiers, counts, hashes and bounded geographic observations are factual review records, not an open dataset license.

Run `python validate-package.py` for offline package checks. It verifies public facts, evidence pins, identifier sequences and stated review limits. It does not independently recheck unpublished full coordinates or raw source bodies. Optional local-cache validation can replay the production decoder and original-source checks; see the geometry and independent-review documentation. Validators perform no downloads.
