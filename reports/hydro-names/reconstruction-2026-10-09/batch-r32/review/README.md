# Independent lake-name review r32

Three new Aleknagik-region inventory targets add three distinct records, taking reconstructed coverage from 90 to 93:

- lakes_base:1159107927: Lake Nerka
- lakes_base:1159110635: Lake Aleknagik
- lakes_base:1159107889: Nunavaugaluk Lake

Each is a bounded generalized water identity in southwestern Alaska, not approval of an entire detailed named-lake extent. Every complete selected source and baseline-rendered polygon part and ring was inspected: one polygon and one exterior ring per target, with 84, 31 and 25 positions including closure, totaling 140. Exact spelling of Nunavaugaluk Lake is readable in two retained labels. Fine arm tips, bay and shore margins, island holes and river connections remain generalized or omitted. Geographic qualifiers and material extent limits accompany each finding.

No new name-scope hold is added. The 26 prior holds and two access-pending targets remain unchanged and were not reassessed. Remaining unrecorded coverage is 3972 targets. This is new inventory research, not restoration of historical verified identities.

Alaska DNR hosts and links the retained map; its TOPO! cartography credits National Geographic and Tele Atlas. Government cartographic authorship and formal naming-registry authority are not established. The map includes land and water outside its drawn park boundary; coverage does not establish park membership. This batch made no external requests or new source acquisitions. The r30 original was reused. Source bibliography and own factual observations appear once in source-observations.json. One cartographic publication supplies all direct naming evidence.

Formal-registry decisions, precise shorelines and name boundaries, Korean forms and scalar product-name application remain unapproved. Product-name fields are null and automatic application is false. No product data or geometry was changed.

Run `python -B validate-independent-review.py` and `python -B test-independent-review.py` in this directory. These bounded current-three checks validate included factual linkage, metadata, arithmetic and included-file integrity. They do not reopen the original map, recompute omitted complete polygons, reproduce visual correspondence or replay the production decoder. Maps, crops, full coordinates, private paths and omitted-input fingerprints are excluded. public-input-pins.json hashes only the nine included geometry-component files. review-manifest.json hashes the seven other review files and excludes itself.
