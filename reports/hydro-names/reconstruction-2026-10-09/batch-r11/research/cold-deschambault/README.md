# Bounded Cold Lake and Deschambault Lake research

Two new actual inventory targets were inspected in full source geometry. Deschambault Lake has supported generalized water identity from the official WSA map and separate ECCC station-name usage. Cold Lake remains unresolved because no usable official map pixels were acquired; its text-only provincial map evidence cannot confirm full-feature correspondence.

Cold Lake is explicitly source-access-limited, its full-feature correspondence review is incomplete, and follow-up is required. It is not counted as a completed identification. Three distinct original-source requests returned HTTP403; no equivalent routes were requested after those responses.

The WSA and ECCC original bytes were reused and hash-checked. Cold Lake has only a retained web-text-extraction hash, not an original PDF-body hash. Source publication dates, original retrieval times, quotation scope and map locators are recorded in source-evidence.json. Original government publications, map images, inspection crops, map-derived vectors and complete extracted texts are excluded.

No formal naming instrument or current registry record was verified. Korean names and whole-feature scalar names remain null; automatic application and product application remain false. No source geometry or product data was changed.
