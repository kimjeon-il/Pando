## Findings

These are distinct component associations, not aliases or a single whole-feature identity. A separately labelled Phillips Lake area is implicated at the generalized northern/northwestern margin. Exact Hatchet-Phillips-Corson divisions, detailed shore/island coverage and complete named-lake extents are not established. Exact extent and product-name application are not approved. Category: `compound_feature_name_scope_hold`.
- **1159111821: Tazin Lake.** The complete selected generalized footprint supports Tazin Lake as a bounded water identity. Fine northern arms, the narrow northeastern/eastern extension, islands/peninsulas and irregular shore margins are generalized or omitted; the entire detailed named lake and exact adjoining-water boundaries are not certified. Exact extent and product-name application are not approved. Category: `supported_generalized_water_identity`.
- **1159112235: Charcoal Lake.** The complete selected generalized footprint supports Charcoal Lake as a bounded water identity. The thin southwestern continuation, part of the southeastern lobe, northern fork details, small branches, islands and inlet/outlet transitions are generalized or omitted; the entire detailed named lake and exact river boundaries are not certified. Exact extent and product-name application are not approved. Category: `supported_generalized_water_identity`.

## Scope and continuity

Generalized identity does not establish surveyed shoreline agreement, exact name partitions, formal registry status or product-ready scalar names. Korean names and whole-feature scalar names remain null; automatic and product application are false.
The index contains 96 distinct targets: 43 river groups and 53 lake features. It preserves 94 non-access-pending records, 2 access-pending records and 27 scope holds. Non-access-pending is an accounting category, not a count of confirmed names. Earlier records and the Richmond/Hastings assessment histories are preserved; the unavailable historical 504-target ledger is not added.
The exact original inventory and cumulative index recover 3,969 unrecorded targets: 3,412 river groups and 557 lakes. Pending IDs and scope holds remain separately recoverable.
