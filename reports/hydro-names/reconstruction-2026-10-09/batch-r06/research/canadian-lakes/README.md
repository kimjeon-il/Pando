# Canadian lake research, r06

Two new actual inventory features have supported generalized lake identities: `lakes_base:1159109553` as **Dore Lake** and `lakes_base:1159110303` as **Ennadai Lake**. Their complete source rings were inspected, retaining all 101 and 192 positions, respectively. No geometry was repaired.

Dore is the exact unaccented form supported by the acquired official evidence. The WSA map was produced November 25, 2014; its 2025 URL directory is not a revised publication date. Ennadai is covered across two adjacent 1979 land-use map sheets, whose base-map information dates to 1963. The mapped North End Lake is a separate waterbody east of the northeastern Ennadai arm.

The map bodies were acquired and visually inspected, including target regions and imprints. Independent government usage is separately supported by the ECCC hydrometric station index and CIRNAC's 2019 Ahiarmiut settlement page. Adjacent sheets and their catalog are not counted as independent confirmations.

These are whole-outline, generalized geographic associations. Fine islands and shoreline details are omitted by the source polygons. Formal current naming status, exact shoreline fit, Korean names and scalar-name approvals are unverified. Both whole-feature scalar-name fields remain null, with automatic and product application false.

`source-evidence.json` distinguishes hashes of exact original HTTP bodies from extracted text and supplies map/image locators. `target-identity-facts.json` pins the inventory and source joins and excludes the prior fifteen targets. `findings.json` preserves the bounded scope and limits. Raw official originals, complete texts, maps and inspection images are excluded from this public package.
