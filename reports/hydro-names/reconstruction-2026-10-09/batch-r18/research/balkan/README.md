# Balkan lake identity review

Three complete generalized polygons were reviewed in fixed order against the locally available one-page PONT Prespa - Ohrid Eco-Region map, printed at 1:340,000. All 120 source positions and all 120 baseline positions were considered.

- `lakes_base:1159116675`, 38 positions: strong geographic correspondence with the western blue-water basin. Lake Ohrid is a low-confidence naming candidate from conservation-overlay wording qualified by re-designate. Whole-waterbody name coverage and naming authority remain unverified.
- `lakes_base:1159118183`, 58 positions: generalized geographic correspondence with the larger eastern blue-water basin. The literal Prespa Lake wording within the Monument of Nature designation supplies a low-confidence naming candidate. It does not directly name the entire water polygon. Great Prespa is unverified.
- `lakes_base:1159118201`, 24 positions: generalized association with the smaller southeastern lake; its naming candidate remains null. The nearby national-park label is not a waterbody name. Small Prespa is unverified.

Geographic shape correspondence is separate from naming confidence. Both printed candidate names occur within partial cross-border conservation overlays; neither is promoted to a supported whole-waterbody identity. The two candidate holds concern name coverage and authority, not a requirement to repair geometry.

PONT is a conservation trust/fund. Its own map is not a governmental naming instrument or registry. Blue water was distinguished from conservation hatching, park outlines, intervention limits and country borders. Comparisons are visual and generalized, not numerical map registration or shoreline surveys. No dependable printed map date was found; neither a URL directory date nor PDF creation metadata is treated as one.

The original map covers all three complete target regions. All three bounded correspondence reviews are complete and none is access-pending. Two UNESCO documents, a Ramsar report and a government management plan were unavailable as usable originals. Their separate acquisition outcomes are recorded, and they supplied no visual evidence.

Follow-up is required for all three targets to establish authoritative name coverage over the complete waterbody. Whole-feature scalar and Korean names remain null; automatic and product application are false. No geometry was changed or historical verdict restored.

This directory contains only bibliography, own findings and selected geometry facts. Original publications, inspection materials, acquisition records and their fingerprints are excluded. Public validation checks included records and geometry; it cannot reproduce the omitted visual map judgment.
