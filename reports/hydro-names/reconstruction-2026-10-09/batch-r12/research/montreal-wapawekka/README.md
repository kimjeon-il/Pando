# Montreal Lake and Wapawekka Lake findings

Both targets have supported generalized waterbody identity from the Saskatchewan Water Security Agency map printed November 25, 2014. The historical review compared the complete 81-position Montreal polygon and 45-position Wapawekka polygon with the mapped waterbodies and neighboring waters. Included source and baseline geometry preserve every ordered position at six-decimal precision.

Montreal's northern inlet and Wapawekka's narrow eastern arm are more detailed on the map than in the generalized polygons. Both full-feature assessments are complete within the bounded identity scope, with no source-access limitation or follow-up needed for that scope. Formal registry status, current naming decisions, exact shoreline and Korean names were not verified. Whole-feature scalar names remain null; automatic and product application remain false.

Both names rely on one cartographic publication at 1:1,750,000. Its official URL, printed date and original retrieval date are recorded in `source-evidence.json`. No new source acquisition was performed. Original publications and inspection materials are absent from this package. Public validation checks the included factual records and selected public-domain lake geometries; it does not repeat the historical visual review.
