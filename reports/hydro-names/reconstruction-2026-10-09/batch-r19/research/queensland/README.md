# Queensland river-group name-scope review

Three actual inventory groups were reviewed in fixed order against the Bureau of Meteorology's Australian Water Resources Assessment 2012, published in 2013. The retained original was reused; no new source acquisition was made.

- `hydro-system:50517279`: generalized Burdekin drainage association. Direct Burdekin channel labels support the main-river corridor. The entire southern feeder was inspected, but its selected-path name remains unverified. Storage, lower river and delta scope are distinguished.
- `hydro-system:50478600`: generalized Gilbert drainage association. The long southeastern upstream path follows the mapped Einasleigh corridor before the lower Gilbert corridor. Both baseline fragments are marked mainstem, which does not establish one name throughout.
- `hydro-system:50464082`: generalized Mitchell drainage association. The eastern modeled mainstem follows the Palmer corridor, and the long southern branch follows the Walsh corridor toward the Mitchell. The shorter northern feeder remains unnamed in this assessment.

All 18 fragments, 552 line parts and 3,360 ordered baseline positions were included in complete-path plots and visual map comparison. The review considered heads, complete branches, junction arrangement, lower courses and coastal endings. It was not a centroid lookup. The comparison is qualitative, with no formal map registration or numerical line-fit.

Direct river labels are distinguished from catchment titles and shading. One official assessment supplies the evidence; its multiple figures are not independent sources or a legal naming register. Coarse map resolution prevents exact per-reach name partitions, tributary transition points, reservoir extents and estuary/distributary boundaries. Original HydroRIVERS coordinate equality was not independently verified.

All three bounded group-name assessments are complete. None is access-pending. No follow-up is required to complete these bounded representative-system assessments. Additional reach-specific evidence would be needed for any separately authorized name application. Whole-group scalar and Korean names are null, all-reach verification is false, and automatic/product application is false. No geometry was modified.

This directory contains six files: bibliography, own findings, bounded geometry identity facts and a manifest of the five other included files. Complete baseline paths, original publications and inspection materials are excluded. Public offline validation cannot reproduce the omitted whole-path and map-pixel comparisons.
