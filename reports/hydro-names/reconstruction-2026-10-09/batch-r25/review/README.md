# Independent river-name review r25

Three selected Northern Territory MAIN_RIV groups were compared in full against retained original Bureau of Meteorology regional maps. The findings distinguish direct river-line names from basin callouts and preserve upper, tidal and coastal naming limits. Mary River here is in Northern Territory, separate from the previously reviewed Queensland Mary River.

Adelaide and Mary have bounded representative identity support. South Alligator retains a geographic naming candidate on a completed available-source scope hold. None has an approved whole-group or every-reach scalar name; Korean names and product names remain null and automatic application is false.

`source-observations.json` is the single public bibliography and own factual extraction. Original maps, complete coordinates and private review materials are omitted. Multiple figures from one publication remain one source group.

`validate-independent-review.py` checks only included metadata and factual linkage. `test-independent-review.py` rejects bounded current-record corruptions. Neither program repeats omitted map interpretation, complete-path comparison or original decoding. A completed scope review may retain a naming hold.
