# Independent lake-name review r30

Three new Wood-Tikchik-region inventory targets add three distinct records, taking reconstructed coverage from 84 to 87:

- lakes_base:1159110649: Upnuk Lake
- lakes_base:1159107913: Chikuminuk Lake
- lakes_base:1159110611: Lake Chauekuktuli

All three support a bounded generalized water identity for the complete selected footprint. Each source and baseline-rendered feature has one polygon and one exterior ring. Every part and ring was inspected; their position counts, including closure, are 18, 40 and 26, totaling 84. No new scope hold or access-pending target is added. The two previous pending-access IDs and 25 previous scope-hold IDs remain unchanged and were not reassessed.

The selected footprint is not certified to contain the entire detailed named lake. Upnuk's mapped narrow western continuation extends beyond the generalized outline. Chikuminuk's islands, fine western branches and terminal river boundary remain unresolved. Chauekuktuli's western inlet details and eastern connections are generalized. Those limits are retained in each finding's supported_name_scope and structured naming_scope_details for downstream use.

Alaska DNR hosts and links the one newly acquired map; its TOPO! cartography carries National Geographic and Tele Atlas credits. Government cartographic authorship and formal naming-registry authority are not established. The official discovery page and repeated map labels do not create independent naming sources. This review made no external requests and reused the original acquired earlier in this batch. Source bibliography and own factual observations are consolidated in source-observations.json.

Formal-registry decisions, precise shorelines and name boundaries, Korean forms and scalar product-name application remain unapproved. Product-name fields are null and automatic application is false. No geometry or product data was changed.

Run `python -B validate-independent-review.py` and `python -B test-independent-review.py` in this directory. These bounded current-three checks validate included factual linkage, metadata, arithmetic and included-file integrity. They do not reopen the original map, recompute omitted complete polygons, reproduce visual correspondence or replay the production decoder. Maps, crops, full coordinates, private paths and omitted-input fingerprints are excluded. public-input-pins.json hashes only the nine included geometry-component files. review-manifest.json hashes the seven other review files and excludes itself.
